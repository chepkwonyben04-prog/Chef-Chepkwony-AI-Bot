Recipe: Kenyan Coconut Fish Curry

Ingredients:
- Fresh fish
- Coconut milk
- Onion
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Tomatoes
- Spices

Method:
Cook the onions, garlic, ginger and spices. Add tomatoes, coconut milk and fish. Simmer until the fish is cooked.

Chef Tip:
Use fresh fish for the best flavour and avoid overcooking seafood.

Recipe: Classic Seafood Pasta

Ingredients:
- Pasta
- Prawns
- Garlic
- Olive oil
- Herbs
- Lemon

Method:
Cook pasta, prepare seafood with garlic and herbs, then combine.

Chef Tip:
Seafood cooks quickly. Remove from heat once perfectly cooked.
